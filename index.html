The K1 Process

Why our floors don't fail.

Most epoxy jobs peel because of one thing: bad prep. We never skip a step.

  1. 01

    Diamond Grind & Prep

    We mechanically grind the slab to open the concrete profile and repair every crack and spall. This is where adhesion is won or lost.

  2. 02

    Base Coat

    A 100%-solids epoxy base is applied to lock into the prepped surface — the foundation for everything above it.

  3. 03

    Metallic Resin Artistry

    Pigmented resin is poured and hand-worked to create the flowing, three-dimensional metallic movement K1 is known for.

  4. 04

    CERAFIN™ Protective Topcoat

    Sealed with Simiron CERAFIN™ for UV stability, chemical resistance, and a finish that holds its shine for years.
