The K1 Process

Why Our Floors Last

A coating is only as good as the prep underneath it. We never shortcut a step — that’s why our floors don’t peel.

  1. 01

    Diamond Grind & Prep

    We mechanically diamond-grind the slab to open the concrete, repair cracks and spalls, and create the perfect profile for a permanent bond.

  2. 02

    High-Build Base Coat

    A pigmented, moisture-tolerant base coat is rolled and back-rolled to seal the concrete and set the foundation color.

  3. 03

    Metallic Resin Pour

    Metallic pigments are hand-worked into the wet resin to create the movement, depth, and one-of-a-kind pattern unique to your floor.

  4. 04

    CERAFIN Topcoat

    We seal it all with Simiron CERAFIN — a ceramic-fortified, UV-stable topcoat that locks in the gloss and shrugs off wear.
